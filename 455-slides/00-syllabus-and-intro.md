# AI Policy — My Personal Stance

<v-clicks>

Theory of computation is a place where **the work _is_ the thinking.**

* If you ask an LLM to do a pumping-lemma proof for you, you've **outsourced the exact skill the quizzes measure.**
* AI is great for:
    * "Explain this lecture concept a different way"
    * "Check whether my proof structure is valid"
* Use it as a **study partner**, not a **ghostwriter**.

</v-clicks>
